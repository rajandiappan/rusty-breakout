### How to Play
1. Press **SPACE** to start

4. Catch falling power-ups
5. Complete all 5 levels to win!

- **M (Gold):** Multi-Ball - Spawn 2 extra balls
- **P (Green):** Paddle Extend - Make paddle bigger
- **S (Purple):** Slow Time - Slow down ball

- Brick: +10 points

- All 5 levels: +5000 points
